Key takeaways

South Africa: a dual burden. It has the world's largest HIV epidemic (17.1% adult prevalence, ~7.8M people living with HIV) and among the highest TB incidence rates globally, while NCDs already cause the majority of deaths (51%) — driven by high obesity and hypertension.
Tanzania: malaria-dominated, NCDs rising fast. Malaria still kills roughly 25,000 people a year — among the highest tolls of any country — while HIV and TB burdens are comparatively lower. NCDs cause about a third of deaths but that share is climbing quickly with urbanisation.
Malaria elimination vs. malaria control. South Africa is nearing elimination (under 10,000 cases, ~106 deaths in 2023, confined to 3 provinces) while Tanzania recorded 3.8 million cases — illustrating two very different points on the malaria control-to-elimination spectrum.
