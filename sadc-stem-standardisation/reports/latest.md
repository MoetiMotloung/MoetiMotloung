## Weakest regional dimensions (priority for intervention)

### Externally Benchmarked Assessments (avg 0.56)
Introduce a voluntary regional STEM benchmark assessment (sampling-based, akin to TIMSS/SACMEQ) that allows systems to compare proficiency without replacing national exams.

- Adopt or expand a sampling-based regional STEM assessment (e.g. aligned to SACMEQ/TIMSS methodology) across all member states
- Publish comparable regional STEM proficiency benchmarks on a recurring cycle
- Use benchmark results to inform national curriculum review cycles

### Accreditation & Quality Assurance (avg 0.81)
Extend SADCQF-style quality assurance mechanisms down to school-level STEM provision, using UNESCO/HAQAA workshops as a model for building shared standards.

- Map existing national school QA bodies and identify common ground for a light-touch regional STEM accreditation standard
- Pilot a voluntary regional accreditation mark for STEM programmes/schools that meet agreed minimum criteria
- Extend HAQAA-style peer-review workshops from higher education down to secondary STEM provision

### Gender Equity in STEM (avg 0.88)
Standardise gender-disaggregated data collection on STEM participation and achievement, and coordinate regional campaigns and targeted financial support as recommended by UNESCO.

- Mandate gender-disaggregated STEM participation/achievement reporting across all member states
- Coordinate a regional girls-in-STEM campaign with shared messaging and materials
- Pool and target scholarship/financial support for girls and women in STEM
